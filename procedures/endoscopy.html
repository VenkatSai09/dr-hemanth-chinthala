Upper GI endoscopy (gastroscopy)

A thin, flexible tube with a camera is passed through the mouth to look at the food pipe, the stomach and the first part of the small intestine (the duodenum). It is the most direct way to find the cause of many upper digestive symptoms.

When it is advised

  • Acidity or heartburn that keeps coming back or does not settle with treatment.
  • Difficulty or pain when swallowing.
  • Vomiting blood, or black, tar-like stools.
  • Iron-deficiency anaemia or weight loss without a clear reason.
  • Ongoing pain in the upper abdomen.
  • Checking for varices (enlarged veins) in people with cirrhosis.
  • Biopsies, for example to test for H. pylori infection or coeliac disease.

Endoscopy can also treat: stopping bleeding, banding varices, removing polyps and stretching narrowings.

How it is done

Your throat is numbed with a spray, and you can also have sedation. The examination itself usually takes 5 to 15 minutes. Biopsies, tiny samples of the lining, are painless.

Before and after

  • Nothing to eat for about six hours before. Follow the hospital's advice about water.
  • Tell the team about blood thinners and diabetes medicines.
  • If you have sedation, do not drive for the rest of the day and bring someone with you.
  • You can eat once the numbness in your throat has worn off, usually after about an hour.

Risks

Diagnostic endoscopy is very safe. Bleeding or a tear in the gut wall are rare, and slightly more likely when a treatment is done during the procedure.

Common questions

Will it hurt?

Most people feel some pressure and the urge to gag for a moment, but not pain. Sedation makes it more comfortable if you are anxious.

When will I know the results?

Dr Chinthala can usually tell you what he saw straight after the test. Biopsy results take a few working days.

Do I need an endoscopy for acidity?

Not always. Many people improve with lifestyle changes and a short course of medicine. Endoscopy is advised when symptoms persist or when there are warning signs such as difficulty swallowing, bleeding or weight loss.
