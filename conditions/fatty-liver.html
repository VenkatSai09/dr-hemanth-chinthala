Fatty liver

In fatty liver, fat builds up inside the liver cells. It is very common in India, often causes no symptoms, and is usually found on an ultrasound or a blood test. Caught early, it can usually be reversed.

The two main types

  • MASLD (metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease, previously called NAFLD) is linked with excess weight, especially around the waist, diabetes, high cholesterol and high blood pressure. It also occurs in people who are not overweight, and is common in Indians.
  • Alcohol-related fatty liver is caused by regular drinking. Some people have both.

Why it matters

For many people the fat does no lasting harm. In some, it causes inflammation (steatohepatitis) and scarring (fibrosis), which over years can lead to cirrhosis. Fatty liver is also linked with a higher risk of heart disease and diabetes, so it is a signal to look after your overall health.

Symptoms

Usually none. Some people feel tired or have a dull discomfort under the right ribs.

Tests

  • Blood tests: liver tests, blood sugar and cholesterol.
  • Ultrasound of the abdomen.
  • FibroScan, a quick, painless scan that measures liver stiffness, to check for scarring.
  • Rarely, a liver biopsy.

Treatment

  • Weight loss: losing 5% of your body weight reduces liver fat; losing 7 to 10% can reduce inflammation and scarring.
  • Diet: less sugar, sweet drinks, refined carbohydrates and fried food; more vegetables, pulses and whole grains.
  • Exercise: at least 150 minutes a week of brisk walking or similar.
  • Stop alcohol, especially if there is any scarring.
  • Good control of diabetes, cholesterol and blood pressure.
  • Medicines for selected patients, and regular checks of liver scarring.

Common questions

Can fatty liver be cured?

In its early stages, yes, it can usually be reversed with weight loss, diet, exercise and stopping alcohol. Scarring can also improve, though more slowly.

I am not overweight. Can I still have fatty liver?

Yes. Fatty liver in people of normal weight is common in India, often linked with fat around the waist, diabetes or high cholesterol.

Do I need to stop alcohol completely?

It is strongly advised, and essential if there is any scarring in the liver.
